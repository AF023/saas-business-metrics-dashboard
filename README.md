# 📈 SaaS Customer Retention & Executive Financial Metrics Dashboard

## 📌 Executive Summary
This project delivers an end-to-end analytical framework to evaluate **Customer Retention**, **Churn Rate**, **Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR)**, and **Customer Lifetime Value (LTV)** for a B2B SaaS business model.

Designed for executive decision-making, it transforms raw transactional logs into strategic insights aimed at reducing customer attrition and optimizing subscription pricing tiers.

---

## 💡 Business Problem & Objectives
- **Problem:** High subscription cancellations in entry-level plans impair predictability in monthly cash flow.
- **Goal:** Identify churn patterns across subscription tiers, calculate core financial KPIs, and provide actionable recommendations to increase customer retention.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Methodology
- **Data Wrangling & Analytics:** Python (Pandas, NumPy).
- **KPI Modeling:** Cohort analysis, Churn Rate calculation, MRR aggregation, and LTV estimation.
- **Repository Structure:**
  - `saas_metrics_analysis.ipynb`: Complete data modeling and metric computation notebook.
  - `saas_customer_metrics.csv`: Processed analytical dataset.

---

## 📊 Key Financial Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- **Active Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR):** Dynamic aggregation of ongoing customer subscriptions.
- **Average Revenue Per User (ARPU):** Standardized revenue distribution across customer accounts.
- **Churn Rate by Plan:** Comparative breakdown highlighting high-risk customer segments.
- **Customer Lifetime Value (LTV):** Financial estimation of long-term account viability.

---

## 🎯 Strategic Business Recommendations
1. **Focus on Basic Tier Onboarding:** Entry-level accounts show higher early churn; implementing guided onboarding reduces 30-day cancellation rates.
2. **Upsell to Annual Contracts:** Incentivize quarterly or annual commitments for mid-tier accounts to secure stable cash flow.
3. **Proactive Account Health Monitoring:** Establish trigger alerts for low feature usage prior to renewal cycles.

---

## 🚀 How to Run
Open `saas_metrics_analysis.ipynb` in Google Colab or any Jupyter Notebook environment and execute all cells sequentially.
